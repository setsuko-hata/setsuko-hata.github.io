# Does any of this sound familiar?

- You wake up feeling tired or stiff, especially around your neck, shoulders, or back.
- Your mind jumps straight to your to-do list, and it’s hard to focus on one thing at a time.
- You spend hours sitting and wish you moved more throughout the day.
- Small stresses pile up, and you want to respond with more patience.
- You’ve tried yoga or meditation on your own, but it’s been hard to keep going.

A regular class gives you a time to show up, a teacher to guide you, and people to practice alongside. If you miss a day, you can come back for the next class.

# What might begin to change?

- Move with more ease. Simple Upa Yoga movements engage your joints and muscles and can help ease stiffness after sleeping or sitting for a long time.
- Feel more awake. Gentle movement can help you feel refreshed and ready to get moving after a period of inactivity.
- Give yourself a moment before you react. Paying attention to your breath gives you a way to practice pausing when you feel rushed or stressed.
- Bring more focus to what you’re doing. Practice giving one movement your full attention, then bring that same attention to a task or a conversation.
- Build a habit you can count on. Showing up for yourself regularly can help you build confidence in your ability to follow through.
