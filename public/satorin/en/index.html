ABOUT HATA SETSUKO

Hello, I’m Setsuko Hata.

Sadhguru Gurukulam-certified Hatha Yoga teacher · Nutritionist · Jazz singer / composer

I spent around a year and a half at the Isha Yoga Center in South India, completing more than 1,750 hours of rigorous Hatha Yoga teacher training. Before that, I worked as a professional musician in the United States for around 13 years, based in New York, Colorado, and Los Angeles.

Having struggled with my physical and mental wellbeing since I was young, I spent many years asking how I could live with greater freedom and joy. Yoga and meditation transformed my life at its roots.

Not only my body, but my state of mind, relationships, approach to work, and life itself began to change. That is why I want to share yoga and meditation as tools for each person to open up the possibilities of their own life.

Why I named this community
“SATORIN”

Notice what your body is telling you. Become aware of your emotions and thoughts. Choose your own life instead of being carried along by others. As each person opens their sensitivity and begins to live more consciously, that change reaches family, friends, and society.

“SATORIN” brings together the Japanese word satori, meaning awakening, and rin, a circle that connects people. Awakening is not a distant state reserved for a special few, but a growing awareness of what is within us. Rather than keeping that awareness to ourselves, we can share it with others and let it spread like a widening circle. This is the wish behind the name.

I named this community SATORIN with the hope of widening this circle of people awakening to themselves.

Let’s practice, learn, and connect,
and open up the possibilities of our lives together.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I join if I am new to yoga? ＋

Yes, beginners are very welcome. Each movement and breathing practice is explained with care. Even if you feel stiff or are not used to exercise, you can practice comfortably at your own pace. Please ask whenever you have a question.

Should I have my camera on or off? ＋

Please keep your camera on so the teacher can see your movements and support correct practice. Position your camera so that your whole body is visible.

Is it okay if I cannot attend every day? ＋

Yes. Join on the days that work for you, even if you cannot attend daily. Continue at a manageable pace and gradually make yoga part of your everyday routine.

Are recordings available? ＋

Sessions are not recorded. We want everyone to feel comfortable joining with their camera on. Please enjoy the time we share together live.

Can I cancel or pause my membership? ＋

You may cancel or pause at any time. Please note that fees already paid for the current month are non-refundable.

What equipment do I need? ＋

A yoga mat and a willingness to give it a try are all you need. Come as you are.
